  • Bauble smash is Day 2 so people can play it well before Christmas.
  • Tabletop mini golf (Day 6) and the tabletop racing circuit (Day 12) share a photo-to-surface engine: build it once.

  • I joined a gym is Day 29 so it lands right before New Year. The Amiga emulator is last because it is the biggest build and gets the most pre-build time.
  • Chase HQ, Ikari, Super Off Road and Hostages are titled as working names. Ship them under original names and art.
  • 13 slots are open. Each has a suggested type so the 30 cover digital, physical and service. Physical products need lead time for stock and shipping.
