How the challenge came about

I keep a long list of product ideas, and most of them stay on the list. Planning and polishing can feel productive, but only shipping tells you whether an idea works. 30 Products 30 Days is a way to force the issue: one finished product a day for a month, built in the open, starting 28 November 2026.

The rules are simple. Each day produces one product that someone can use, whether digital, physical or a service. Small and finished beats big and perfect. If you want to run your own 30 days, you are welcome to join in.

Who I am

I'm Malcolm Gordon, a builder based in Perth, Western Australia. I make websites, tools and games as personal projects, and I collect them on my portfolio site.
