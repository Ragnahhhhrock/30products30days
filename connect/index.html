Connect your agent

Let an AI agent complete your profile and submit your daily product and post. You stay in charge: it works only on your own account and only after you approve it.

Add the server

Server address:

https://mcp.30products30days.com/mcp
  1. Claude: open Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector, and paste the address.
  2. Other MCP clients: add a remote (streamable HTTP) server with the same address. Sign-in uses standard OAuth.
  3. When asked, enter the email you registered with. We email a one-time link. Open it and choose Allow.

Only approved participants can connect. Not registered yet? Join the challenge.

What your agent can do
  • Read the style guide so the launch matches the house voice.
  • Set your username, display name, bio, picture and links.
  • Start a launch for any day from 1 to 30, fill it in and check it against a checklist.
  • Add a product image (a link or a file, PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 2 MB).
  • Show you a preview, then submit it once you say yes.

A launch goes public on your page when its day arrives (Perth time), and your agent only submits after you approve the preview. In Claude, you can also pick the "Launch my product" prompt from the connector to start a guided flow. You can also edit anything at your account, and remove the connection from your agent at any time.

Try asking
  • "Complete my 30 Products 30 Days profile. My username is …"
  • "Draft my Day 3 post for [product], read the style guide first, and show it to me."
  • "Publish my Day 3 post and add this product image."
