Day 2 is **Bauble Smash**: a free Christmas tap-smash game that runs in your phone's browser. It is for anyone who wants a quick, festive game to play in a spare minute.

## How it plays
- Tap the baubles on the tree to smash them before they disappear.
- Red baubles score 1, golden baubles score 5 and rare green Grinch baubles score 10.
- Snowmen give you a life back. Leave the coal and the dark, dull baubles alone.
- Climb through the levels and beat your personal best.

## What else is in there
- Pause and pick up again whenever you need a breather.
- World and "My device" leaderboards, with your name on the board if you make the top scores.
- A global counter of every bauble smashed around the world.

## What it costs
Free. No download, no sign-up. If it makes you smile, there is a "Buy me a cup of Hot Cocoa" button.

Tap. Smash. Sparkle.
