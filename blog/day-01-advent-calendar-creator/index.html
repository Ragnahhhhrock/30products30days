Day 01: Advent calendar creator

Turn your photos into a 24-door digital advent calendar with snow and stickers.

Day 1 is Advent calendar creator: a free way to turn your own photos into a digital advent calendar for Christmas 2026. It is for anyone who wants to give someone a calendar that is personal, not a box of chocolate.

What it does

  • Pick up to 24 photos from your camera roll, or take new ones.
  • Auto-decorate adds a Christmas filter, a frame and stickers to suit each photo, and you can tweak anything.
  • Door 1 opens on 1 December and door 24 on Christmas Eve. Doors stay locked until their day.
  • Three looks to pick from: Midnight, Hearth and Pop Print.

What it costs

Free. No sign-up. Your photos stay on your device and nothing is uploaded.

One thing to know: calendars live on the device that made them for now, so use the same phone or browser each day. Sharing by link is on the list.
