Day 00: We're an official West Tech Fest satellite event

30 Products 30 Days is now listed as an official West Tech Fest 2026 satellite event.

Big news two days before kickoff: 30 Products 30 Days is now an official satellite event of West Tech Fest 2026.

West Tech Fest celebrates Western Australia's startup ecosystem: innovation, investment and entrepreneurship. Satellite events are the community-run events that sit alongside the main festival, and we're now listed among them.

What's happening

  • Starts: Saturday 28 November 2026, running through festival week
  • Where: online, free to follow
  • The challenge: one product made and launched every day for 30 days
  • The products: digital, physical or services, most designed and built with Claude

Every day gets its own launch post here, with a live demo link so you can try the product yourself.

Follow along or join in

  • See the listing: West Tech Fest satellite events
  • Join the challenge: register here
  • Watch the advent calendar for each day's door

Thanks to the West Tech Fest team for including us. Day 1 lands on Saturday!
